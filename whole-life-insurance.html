Whole life insurance

Permanent coverage for people comparing more than just short-term protection.

Whole life insurance is often part of the conversation when the goal is long-term family protection, legacy planning, or coverage designed to stay in place for life instead of ending after a term period.

Whole life FAQ

Answers that help people understand permanent coverage faster.

Is whole life the same as term life?

No. Term life is temporary. Whole life is part of a permanent life insurance conversation and is usually compared for different long-term goals.

Should I still compare term life first?

In many cases, yes. Some people start with term life and then compare whether a permanent option like whole life belongs in the plan too.

What should I include in my first text?

Your age range and whether you are mainly focused on lifelong coverage, family stability, or legacy planning.

Can I compare whole life against IUL?

Yes. That is a normal comparison for people looking at more permanent life insurance directions.
