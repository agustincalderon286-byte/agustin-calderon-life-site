IUL FAQ

Answers that help visitors get oriented before they text.

Is IUL the same as term life?

No. Term life is temporary coverage for a set period. IUL is part of a permanent life insurance conversation and is usually compared for different long-term goals.

Do I need to know all the details before reaching out?

No. Many people text first because they want help understanding whether IUL even belongs in the conversation.

What should I include in my first text?

Your age range, whether you are comparing against term life, and whether your main goal is lifetime protection, flexibility, or legacy planning.

Can I still keep the process simple?

Yes. The point of the text-first flow is to make a more advanced topic feel easier to start.
