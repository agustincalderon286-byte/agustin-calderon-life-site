Is mortgage protection the same as term life?

Sometimes the conversation leads to term life because term coverage is often a practical way to protect the years when the mortgage matters most.

What should I include in my first message?

Your age range, rough mortgage situation, and whether your main goal is covering the balance, replacing income, or protecting the monthly payment burden.

Yes. Some visitors start with mortgage protection and then compare broader family protection or permanent life insurance options too.
