Is universal life the same as IUL?

Not exactly. They are both part of a broader permanent life insurance conversation, but people often compare them separately when they want more clarity.

Can I compare universal life and whole life too?

Yes. That is a normal comparison for people looking at permanent coverage options.

What should I include in my first text?

Your age range and whether your main goal is long-term family protection, flexibility, or legacy planning.
