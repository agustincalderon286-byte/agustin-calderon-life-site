Business owners FAQ

Answers that help owners move toward a real quote faster.

What kind of policy do business owners compare first?

Many start with term life because it can provide larger protection around the years when income, debt, and business pressure are strongest.

Can I compare IUL or universal life too?

Yes. Some owners want to compare more permanent options alongside term life, especially when they are thinking longer term.

What should I text first?

Your age range, what kind of business you run, and whether your top concern is family protection, debt, or business continuity pressure.
