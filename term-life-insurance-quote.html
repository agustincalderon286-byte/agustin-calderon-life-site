Term life FAQ

Answers that support better quote conversations.

How much term life should I compare?

A common starting point is income replacement, debt payoff, and future household expenses. The right amount depends on what your family would need if you were not there to provide income.

Is term life only for young parents?

No. It can also make sense for homeowners, business owners, or anyone who wants protection during specific financial years.

What should I text first?

Your age range, what coverage amount you are thinking about, and whether your main goal is family protection, mortgage coverage, or replacing income.

Can I compare term life against IUL or permanent options?

Yes. Many people start with term life and then compare whether a permanent option should also be part of the plan.

Do people in Chicago usually start with term life?

Often, yes. It is a common first comparison for families and homeowners who want substantial protection while keeping the monthly cost more manageable.
