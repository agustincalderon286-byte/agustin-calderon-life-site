Term life vs whole life: a practical first comparison.

Quick difference

Term is usually about cost and coverage size. Whole life is usually about permanence.

That is the simplest first distinction. From there, the right fit depends on how long you want coverage, what you want to protect, and how much monthly budget you want to commit.

Side-by-side

The difference most people care about first.

Term life insurance

  • Coverage for a set number of years
  • Often lower monthly cost
  • Common for mortgages, children, and income replacement

Whole life insurance

  • Designed as permanent coverage
  • Usually a different monthly cost profile
  • Common for long-term protection and legacy goals

Comparison FAQ

Answers that help people choose the right first conversation.

Is term life cheaper than whole life?

In many cases, yes. That is one reason people often start with term when they want a larger amount of protection at a more manageable cost.

Does whole life last longer than term life?

Yes. Whole life is designed as permanent coverage, while term life usually covers a specific number of years.

Which one is better for family protection?

Both can play a role, but many families begin with term life because it may make larger protection amounts easier to compare.

Can I compare both by text?

Yes. Text Agustin with your age range, budget direction, and whether your priority is affordability, permanent protection, or legacy planning.
