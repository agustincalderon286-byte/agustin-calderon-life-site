Common questions

Answers that help visitors move forward with confidence.

How much life insurance do I need?

Many people start by considering income replacement, debts, children’s needs, and future household expenses. The right amount depends on your situation.

What is the difference between term and whole life?

Term life covers a specific time period. Whole life is designed to last for life and is often chosen for long-term planning or legacy goals. Some clients also compare universal life or IUL options depending on flexibility and goals.

Can I get coverage if I already have health concerns?

In many cases, yes. Options vary by age, health profile, and the type of policy you apply for.

What kinds of policies can I ask about by text?

You can text about term life, final expense, indexed universal life, universal life, and other permanent protection options.
